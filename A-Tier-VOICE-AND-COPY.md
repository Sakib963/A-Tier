# A-Tier — Voice & Copy Deck

The humor guide and ready-to-use copy for every surface. This is not decoration — **every text, icon, and empty state should carry the Atiar spirit.** Treat these strings as the default; the agent should use them (or write in this voice) rather than shipping generic labels.

---

## DO NOT USE EM DASH (—) IN ANY TEXT

## 1. The voice in one line

A-Tier is an affectionate tribute to **Atiar bhai** — the most spontaneous, can-do colleague alive, a man who will fix the water pump, ship the software, and extend the office building, all by today, all at 100% confidence. Every screen treats him as a folk legend.

---

## 2. Principles (read before writing a single word)

**Do:**
- **Celebrate, always.** The joke is "Atiar bhai is unstoppable," never "Atiar bhai is foolish." He's the hero of every line.
- **Punch up the legend, never down at a person.** Not Atiar, not the staff who play free, not whoever owes money. Everyone is an Atiar of some grade.
- Keep it warm and insider — this is a love letter to a great colleague, written by friends who take fun in a good way.
- Light Banglish and "bhai" are welcome and land well. Keep it readable.
- Give reusable strings (loaders, empty states) a small rotation so it never feels copy-pasted.

**Don't:**
- Never mock, belittle, or make anyone the butt of the joke — least of all Atiar.
- Never let a debtor feel shamed. Negative balances get gentle, funny nudges, not scolding.
- **Never let comedy obscure a real number.** On any screen with money, the figure is instantly clear; the humor lives in the label around it. Clarity beats comedy on money screens, every time.

---

## 3. The Atiar canon (the running gags to draw from)

- **Says yes to everything.** "Atiar bhai already said yes." "Consider it approved."
- **Can do literally anything** — plumbing, backend, frontend, construction, catering, HR, the water pump, the office extension.
- **Everything takes 2 days.** Or today. Or "give me 10 minutes." The estimate is always heroic.
- **Confidence is permanently 100%.**
- **Spontaneous** — decisions happen instantly, no meeting required.
- **The name is the brand.** A-Tier = Atiar. He doesn't have a tier. He *is* the tier.

Pull from these; don't use them all at once.

---

## 4. Recurring visual motifs & icons

Use a small, consistent motif set so the brand reads instantly:
- **Wrench** — the "fixes anything / water pump" gag. Good for settings, admin, fix-it actions.
- **Hard hat** — the "extends the office building" gag. Good for anything under construction / coming soon.
- **Whistle / football** — match and referee surfaces; the logo can fuse an "A" with a football.
- **"YES" stamp** — approvals, confirmations.
- **"100%" badge** — a permanent Confidence Meter component, pinned near every deadline, always at 100%.
- **"≤ 2 DAYS" stamp** — near timelines and countdowns.
- **Mascot (optional):** a stylized *illustrated* figure in a jersey holding a wrench in one hand and a laptop in the other — the can-do-anything silhouette. **No real photos of any person** — monogram or illustration only.

Logo/wordmark: **A-Tier** with the "A" doing double duty as a football or a whistle. Tagline sits under it (see §5).

## 6. Tier & status naming

Functional spec stays **Full / Half / Free** everywhere in data and logic. The *displayed* skin:

| Data value | Displayed as | Note |
|-----------|--------------|------|
| full | Full Atiar | pays a full share |
| half | Semi-Atiar | pays half |
| free | Honorary Atiar | plays on the house (staff) — a title of honor, never a jab |

Everyone is an Atiar of some grade. That's the whole point.

---

## 7. The one hard rule

On any screen showing real money — a balance, what someone owes, the per-head rate, the finalize preview — **the number is unmissable and correct, and the joke never gets in its way.** Humor decorates the label; it never hides the figure. If a line ever makes the amount harder to read, cut the line.
